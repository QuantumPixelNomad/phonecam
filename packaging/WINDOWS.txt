Phone Cam for Windows
=====================

Use your Android phone's camera and microphone as a webcam and mic.

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Install OBS Studio (free): https://obsproject.com
   Phone Cam uses OBS's virtual camera driver. You don't need to open OBS.
2. For the microphone (optional), install VB-CABLE (free): https://vb-audio.com/Cable
   Run its setup as administrator, then restart Windows.
3. On the phone: Settings > About phone > tap "Build number" 7 times,
   then Developer options > turn on "USB debugging".

USE
1. Run PhoneCam.exe.
2. Connect the phone by USB (accept "Allow USB debugging?" on the phone),
   or over Wi-Fi with Pair... / Connect.
3. Pick the camera and press Start.
4. In Discord, Zoom, Teams, Meet...:
     Camera:     OBS Virtual Camera
     Microphone: CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)

Nothing is installed on the phone. scrcpy and adb are included in this folder.
